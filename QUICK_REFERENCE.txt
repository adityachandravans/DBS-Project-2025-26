   URL: http://localhost/your-project/
   Action: Click "Get Started" or "Register"
   ↓

Step 2: REGISTRATION
   URL: http://localhost/your-project/auth/register.php
   Fill: Username, Email, Password
   Action: Click "Create Account"
   ↓ AUTOMATIC REDIRECT ↓

Step 3: OTP VERIFICATION
   URL: http://localhost/your-project/auth/verify_otp.php
   ✅ Success message appears
   📧 Check email for 6-digit OTP
   Action: Enter OTP and click "Verify OTP"
   ↓ AUTOMATIC REDIRECT ↓

Step 4: LOGIN
   URL: http://localhost/your-project/auth/login.php
   ✅ "Email verified successfully!" message appears
   Fill: Email, Password
   Action: Click "Sign In"
   ↓ AUTOMATIC REDIRECT ↓

Step 5: DASHBOARD
   URL: http://localhost/your-project/dashboard.php
   ✅ Welcome message with your username
   ✅ Statistics displayed
   ✅ Quick action buttons

Step 6: MANAGE PRODUCTS
   - View Products: products/index.php
   - Add Product: products/add.php
   - Edit Product: products/edit.php?id=X
   - Delete Product: products/delete.php?id=X

Step 7: LOGOUT
   Action: Click "Logout" from any page
   ↓ AUTOMATIC REDIRECT ↓
   Returns to: index.php (Home Page)
   ✅ Session destroyed

⚡ QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: Can't access any page
Fix: Start Apache and MySQL in XAMPP

Problem: Database error
Fix: Import database.sql in phpMyAdmin

Problem: Email not sending
Fix: Configure php.ini and sendmail.ini (see EMAIL_SETUP_GUIDE.txt)

Problem: OTP not received
Fix: Check spam folder, verify email config, test with test_email.php
